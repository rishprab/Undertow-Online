UNDERTOW

A trick-dodging card game for 3–6 sailors

The goal

Every trick you take is a point, and points are bad. The game ends at the end of the round in which someone's ship sinks (reaches the limit below). The lowest score wins.

Playing a trick

Whoever holds the 0 opens the first trick of each round with it (with 3 sailors, the 11). Play goes round the table, one card each.

Following: if you hold a card of any suit already in the trick, you must play one of them. If you can't, play anything, which adds a new suit to the trick.

The game lights up only the cards you're allowed to play.

Reading your cards: the column of small dots on each card shows where it sits in its suit, with the big gold dot marking this card (top dot = just below the Captain). Tap any card, even when it's not your turn, and the gangplank slides out above your hand with every number in that suit: your card in gold, your other cards in cream, the Captain starred. If the host turns on Cross out played cards in the lobby, cards already played this round are slashed out too, and the gangplank counts how many higher cards are still out there.

Who takes the trick

The suit with the most cards in the trick wins, and the highest card of that suit takes it. If suits tie for most cards, the highest card among the tied suits takes it.

Coral 14, Coral 17, Glacier 36, Glacier 33, Glacier 38 → Glacier has 3 cards to Coral's 2, so the 38 takes it.
Coral 14, Coral 17, Glacier 36, Glacier 33 → 2 each, so the highest of them, the 36, takes it.

So a big number is dangerous only if its suit ends up the biggest group. The “If you play this” box (the host can switch it on in the lobby) shows who would take the trick right now if you played the card you've picked.

The trick-taker leads the next trick with any card.

Captains

If you take a trick with a Captain, the point still counts, but you choose who leads the next trick. Use it to put a rival on the spot.

Playing online

One person creates a game and shares the 4-letter code or QR code. Everyone plays on their own phone or laptop, and your hand is private. Tap a card to pick it, then tap it again or press Play.

With a shared TV: on the TV or a laptop everyone can see, open the game and choose Use this screen as the table. It shows a big QR code to join, then the whole table: every sailor's seat, the trick, the fleet of sinking ships and (if crossing out is on) a ship's log of every card played this round. Phones become your hand plus a small view of the current trick. The first sailor to join is the lead: they, or the TV, can set sail and start a new game. Between rounds the next one deals itself after 15 seconds, or the lead can deal straight away. The "if you play this" preview is switched off in TV games.

Sound: card snaps, trick sweeps, a Captain's fanfare and a ship's bell. With a TV only the TV plays sound; otherwise every device does. Use the sound button to mute.

If someone drops out, they can open the game again to rejoin. While they're away, a card is played for them after about 20 seconds so the game can keep going.
